Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 26, 2026
Version: 1.0.0

1. Data We Collect

  • Discord user IDs (for access control and preferences)
  • Server IDs (for configuration and settings)
  • Messages sent directly to the bot (processed in real-time, not stored)

2. How We Use Data

  • Process your requests to generate AI responses
  • Maintain user preferences and settings
  • Improve bot functionality and performance
  • Provide personalized assistance based on context

3. Data Retention

Messages are processed in real-time and not stored permanently. User/server IDs are retained only while the bot is active in your server. All data is stored securely and encrypted at rest.

4. Third-party Services

The bot may use third-party AI services to process your requests. These services may have their own privacy policies, but we ensure they adhere to data protection standards.

5. Your Rights

  • You can request deletion of your data at any time
  • You can remove the bot from your server to stop data collection
  • You can contact us to access any data we have about you
